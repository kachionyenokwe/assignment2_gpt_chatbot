# MetroCity Smart Infrastructure Knowledge Base & Support FAQ

## 1. Traffic Light Operations & Emergency Overrides
- **Standard Phase Timings**: Urban intersections operate on dynamic cycle lengths between 45s and 120s based on queue depth sensors.
- **Priority Vehicles**: Emergency vehicles (Ambulance, Fire, Police) transmit a 5.9 GHz DSRC signal that triggers an automatic green-wave override within 3 seconds.
- **Pedestrian Crossing Logic**: Pedestrian walk signals trigger automatically when queue wait time exceeds 90 seconds or when physical pushbuttons are activated.
- **Nighttime Mode**: Between 23:00 and 05:00, low-volume intersections switch to flashing yellow (main road) and flashing red (side road).

## 2. Power Grid & Microgrid Peak Demand Rules
- **Peak Pricing Hours**: High demand grid rates apply weekdays between 14:00 and 19:00.
- **Standby vs Active Power Draw**: Smart LED signal heads consume 5.0 kW in standby (RED) and up to 15.0 kW during active high-capacity signal switching (GREEN).
- **EV Charging Load Shedding**: During Stage 2 grid warnings (demand > 85%), public EV fast-charging stations are capped at 50% max output capacity.
- **Microgrid Storage**: Solar-battery storage buffers at municipal intersections provide up to 6 hours of continuous backup operation during grid outages.

## 3. Citizen Incident Reporting & Support Policies
- **Reporting Signal Failures**: Citizens can report dark or malfunctioning signals via the SmartCity Mobile App or by calling 311 (Ext 4).
- **Pothole & Road Hazard Claims**: Damage claims must be submitted within 30 days of the incident, supported by time-stamped photo evidence and location coordinates.
- **Permit Processing**: Heavy transport permits for oversized vehicles require 48-hour advance notice for automated signal height adjustments along designated corridors.
